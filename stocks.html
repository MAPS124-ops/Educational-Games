Step-by-Step Guide to Buying & Selling Equities

Step 1: Opening a Long Position (Buying Shares)

When you buy shares of a company like Apple (AAPL) or Nvidia (NVDA), you own fractional equity in that corporation. Your total cost equals Share Price × Quantity.

Example Order Calculation:

10 Shares of MSFT @ $420.00/share = $4,200.00 deducted from your Buying Power.

Step 2: Closing a Position & Capital Gains Math

You lock in a profit (or loss) when you SELL your shares back to the market.

Capital Gain Formula:

Profit = (Sell Price − Average Buy Price) × Shares Sold

If you bought 10 shares at $100 ($1,000) and sold at $125 ($1,250), your realized capital gain is +$250.00 (+25%).
